Butchart Gardens is a stunning attraction just outside Brentwood Bay BC. Jennie Butchart started the garden 122-years ago with the help of her husband to beautify an old limestone quarry and their surrounding estate. The quarry was turned into the beautiful and inspiring “Sunken Garden.” Their tennis courts were also turned into the vibrant “Italian Garden.” And the Japanese gardener Isaburo Kishida was invited to design the “Japanese Garden” next to Butchart Cove.

Butchart Gardens sports six separate gardens all with their own stunning aesthetics, as well as a plethora of fountains, statues, two restaurants, and other attractions such as the “Rose Carousel” or a live concert stage. And after you're done viewing all the flowers, you can have a look at the gift shop where you can purchase seeds for all the plants you've been admiring or a souvenir to give to your family back home
