Sustainability

Sustainability

All these plants are not just for show; the Butchart Gardens takes great efforts in being a sustainable, eco friendly attraction. Turning plant waste into compost, using natural materials such as wood utensils, and not using chemical pesticides or herbicides in favour of manual or mechanical methods are just some of the things that Butchart Gardens does to be more sustainable.

The facilities around the park are also cleaned using natural or eco friendly cleaners, and many ingredients used in the various food attractions are sourced from either the local community or from the herb garden on the Butchart estate. Extra packing materials are also reused or recycled in the gift shop, and most packaging is either cardboard or recycled plastic. Sustainability is not just limited to the functions of the park as staff are also encouraged and rewarded for carpooling to work and all visitors that bring dogs are provided with free, biodegradable waste bags.
