Residents/Staff

Staff

The staff care for the garden year-round, weeding, watering, pruning and replanting when needed. The staff also run guided tours around the garden, two gift shops, two restaurants, a gelalateria, and so much more.

Residents

But there is more to the garden than the staff. Around the garden you can find hundreds of bees working tirelessly to pollinate the flora around the grounds. Hopping from roses to false spirea these loyal subjects stand out against the royal shades that embody much of the garden, making them perfect for all your photos.
